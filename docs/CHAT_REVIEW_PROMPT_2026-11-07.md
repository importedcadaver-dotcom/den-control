She is publicly cuddly and genuinely loving toward subjects, but **possessive** (views them as family/property), ruthless when rejected. **The previous monarch died unexpectedly**, causing her accession. That person's identity, relationship to Ursula, and manner of death are UNDECIDED. Ursula's appearance, palace, private history and daily routines remain to be designed individually.

**Required audit outputs:**
A. Brief **executive finding**, including whether the project has a working source/build and when GitHub last changed, with source timestamps.
B. **Coverage statement:** messages/documents/branches/commit history actually inspected; uncertainties.
C. A **canon ledger** split into approved vs proposed vs rejected vs unanswered, with precise correction priority.
D. A **four-column issue table**: issue/priority | evidence and source | impact | concrete next action. Include contradictions, overreaching assistant assumptions, missing features, time/age/calendar logic, law-and-power incoherence, childcare, NPC identity, and technical risks.
E. **Historical error audit**: find previous AI mistakes including unwanted invented 20-person cast, outdated Round 5 selection supersessions, claims of completed builds not supported by repo, UI rendering failure from the giant Round 6 questionnaire, and any other verified mistakes; acknowledge uncertainty instead of inflating error counts.
F. **GitHub inventory**: real files/extensions, missing code/assets/tests/releases/PRs/issues if verified, and last 5–10 commits with exact dates and purpose. Do not equate commit count with coding progress.
G. **System design coverage**: gate + refuge, home/childcare, school, careers, HM economy/debt, law/government, media, criminal justice, city layout, aging, NPC persistence, consequences, UI/settings and cheating/saves. Identify blockers to an actual first playable week.
H. **Checkpoint**: current project stage, accepted milestone, deferred or missing decisions, next design priority, next implementation priority, and acceptance tests. Do not declare the game ready.
I. **Self-critique of the assistant**: where it had authority vs made assumptions; distinguish inferred psychology from an approved biography and never fabricate motivations or family members.
J. **Next interview**: ask **5–8 substantive questions at once** about **Queen Ursula only** (or a new person if I explicitly authorize that). Include A/B/C/Other choices with recommendations clearly marked as *not approved*. Use a compact text table/list, not a massive interactive form that may fail to render.

Be direct and detailed. **Do not silently repair unapproved items**. If you identify a gap, mark it as an open decision and propose alternatives. Finish with a concise handoff summary that another assistant could use without reading this entire chat.
