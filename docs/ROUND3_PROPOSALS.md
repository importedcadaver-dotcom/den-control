# Unbearable Conditions — Round 3 proposals (resolved / historical)

## Scope and central design principles

- The city is **Honeyhold**, a constitutional monarchy with elected officials, ministries, and competing factions. The Queen's name is undecided; the Queen has approved significant emergency powers within a constitutional monarchy.
- Mama (seven-year-old brown bear) works alternate gate and city-owned Briarwood Refuge management days, with home scenes, school involvement, and a relative caregiver.
- Button and Hazel are six-month-old brown bear littermates. Button is clingy, openly emotional, unfiltered, and eager to help Mama. Hazel is independent, inquisitive, and drawn toward delinquent cub peers at cub school.
- Keep a **shift-level timer only**; no per-person inspection countdown. Exact time progression is pending clarification.
- Outcomes must be observable through individual people, households, schools, legal records, cases, and city institutions, not just headlines or a moral score.
- Citizenship/city-entry permission, refuge placement, legal guardianship, foster placement, criminal arrest, school sanction, and medical custody are separate state transitions. Each needs explicit legal authority and a traceable event log.

## Political design — proposed

A constitutional monarchy with:
1. **Crown**: symbolic unity and potential emergency powers; privately disputed royal prerogatives;
2. **Elected Assembly**: budget, housing, school law, public scrutiny, accountability;
3. **Ministries**: Gate Administration, Civic Welfare / Briarwood oversight, Education, Housing, Justice, Treasury;
4. **Courts / review**: formal warrants, disciplinary appeals, child-protection safeguards;
5. **Independent actors**: unions, newspapers, charities, political movements, business associations, criminal networks.

Avoid a simplistic morally fixed faction chart. Individual policies can help some residents while harming others; a benevolent-sounding policy can conceal corruption. No species should be inherently criminal or virtuous.

### State simulation

Represent each policy as a versioned rule with enactment date, responsible authority, affected groups, effective dates, legal exceptions, and enforcement instructions. Separate legal compliance from player knowledge and morality. A ministry can be corrupt without every employee being complicit.

## Schools and cub life — proposed

**Superseded by Round 3 approval:** 0–3 months remain den-dependent with Mama; nursery/social school begins at 4 months; at 6 months Button and Hazel both attend preschool (roughly human three-year-old developmental equivalent), with separate teachers, friendships and interests. Bears walk on all four legs. See approved canon.

- School has staff, rooms, peer groups, attendance, schoolwork / play, parent meetings, reputation, and disciplinary records.
- Delinquent peer groups need distinct motives and relationships, not a generic 'bad kids' flag. Explore pranks, theft, forbidden districts, contraband, and exploitation through consequences and interpersonal context.
- Hazel's curiosity can create both opportunities (discover inconsistencies in official policy) and trouble (breaking limits). Her independence may strengthen or weaken based on Mama's parenting style and the trust between them.
- Button's emotion and lack of a filter can surface truthful facts, accidentally hurt friends, or spark conflict; he must also show initiative, competence and growth. Do not reduce him to a crying event trigger.
- Avoid rewarding punitive responses by default. Mediation, reparative action, parental advocacy, staff competence, and independent child-protection systems should be meaningful.

## Crime and legal process — proposed

Distinct routes:
1. Ordinary minor offenses and school discipline.
2. Opportunistic crime, local gangs, systemic poverty, and survival choices.
3. Organized Panda Mafia extortion and debt leverage with other factions.
4. Misconduct by gate officers, supervisors, welfare staff, courts, and officials.
5. Political policing and resistance.

The checkpoint does **not** let Mama arbitrarily sentence a cub to prison; entry denial, temporary holding, referral, investigation, arrest, hearing, and sentencing belong to different legal roles and create different gameplay opportunities. Mama can acquire new authority via promotion, but must remain constrained by rules.

Safety and narrative principles: fictional crimes and grim consequences can be portrayed directly; keep violence in narrative/cutscenes rather than modeling real-world operational assassination instructions. Do not provide actionable details for weapons or drug trafficking.

## Economy and family — proposed

Track separate ledgers for:
- Mama household: wages, housing, food, heating, debt, parenting/caregiving expenses;
- publicly funded Briarwood: subsidy, donations, salaries, food, heating, beds, capacity, repairs;
- Crown/city: wages, prices, taxes, shortages, institutional funding.

The relative caregiver has their own needs, personal schedule, judgments, and relationship with each cub. Alternating workdays should matter: on gate days, refuge staff work offscreen; on refuge days, checkpoint cases continue via colleagues. Only meaningful emergencies interrupt.

## Multigenerational continuation — approved premise, mechanics proposed

The player's selected ending path includes Mama infiltrating the bear capitol, killing the queen, being martyred, and a revolution cutscene, then continuing play as Button and Hazel. The heirs must be persistent characters before the event. Suggested continuation mechanics: player alternates between two siblings, their distinct goals and disagreements persist, family/guardianship records update, and the revolution changes laws, factions, and institutions rather than just changing scenery.

**Superseded by Round 3 approval:** following Mama's death, the player chooses Button **or** Hazel as the main playable sibling; the other continues autonomously. Time skip, outcome, and endgame scope remain undecided.

## Suggested next acceptance tests

1. In a school arc, Hazel's choices change specific peer ties and a teacher's understanding; Button can disclose something truthful without forcing the player down one scripted path.
2. A government rule change modifies the actual gate rulebook and case validator, an NPC's treatment, and later case review.
3. Mama's checkpoint and refuge schedules alternate, while delegated staff behavior persists across days.
4. A detention, guardianship change, or adoption is represented as a properly authorized transition, survives saving, and cannot be overwritten by a generic story event.
5. After an ending that removes Mama, both children remain in the world with separate agency; relationships, history, and social context continue correctly.
6. Newspaper claims can be disputed by evidence; reporting does not automatically reveal objective truth.
