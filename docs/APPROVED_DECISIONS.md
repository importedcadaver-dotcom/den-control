# Den Control — Approved decisions

**Authority:** Explicit user choices in ChatGPT, November 6, 2026. Do not silently treat assistant recommendations as canon. Unresolved issues are listed separately.

## Round 1 — approved

1. Main checkpoint is at a **major cave-city gate**, with **Briarwood Refuge as a separate location**.
2. Mama begins as an **admissions officer** and can later gain authority, assignments, or promotions.
3. Mama begins with **two fully authored cubs**, each with a distinct personality.
4. Mama's government checkpoint role also makes her responsible for **operating Briarwood Refuge**.
5. Presentation: **detailed 2D pixel art**, building on the existing Godot Den Control prototype.
6. The day advances through **flexible, player-controlled phases**, not a strict real-time timer.
7. A **branching 25+ day campaign**, followed by continued free play, with possible endings and consequences.
8. This is a **completely independent canon** from the user's other bear-game universes.

## Round 2 — approved from the user's screenshot

1. **A**: Briarwood Refuge is **city-owned and publicly funded**. Mama manages its staff, finances, and residents.
2. **A**: Mama's two cubs are **biological littermates**, the **same age**, with very different personalities.
3. **A**: A **relative** looks after the cubs while Mama works. The relative's identity and characterization are not yet confirmed.
4. **C**: Mama works **alternating checkpoint days and refuge-management days**. Do **not** impose both full shifts on the same day.
5. **A**: Mama initially has **limited formal legal exceptions**, with penalties for unauthorized actions.
6. **C**: The cave-city is a **constitutional monarchy**, with elected officials, ministries, and competing factions.
7. **B**: **Frequent moral tension**, everyday life between crises, and **occasional extreme shocks**.
8. **B**: **No inspection deadlines**. Only the broader daily schedule matters. In particular, **do not import the HTML prototype's 120-second timer**.

## Character, identity, and ending choices — approved

- **Game title:** *Unbearable Conditions*. The GitHub repository may remain named `den-control`.
- **Cave-city:** **Honeyhold**.
- **Mama:** Female **brown bear**, **7 years old**, mother of the two cubs.
- **Button:** Male **brown bear**, **6 months old**. Biological littermate of Hazel. Extremely clingy and outwardly emotional; says what he thinks without a filter even when it hurts feelings; loves helping Mama.
- **Hazel:** Female **brown bear**, **6 months old**. Biological littermate of Button. Fiercely independent, curious, inquisitive, often asks "why" and insists on doing things herself; beginning to mix with delinquent cubs at cub school.
- **School:** Hazel attends cub school and is beginning to associate with delinquent cubs. Exact structure, calendar and attendance rules are undecided.
- **Newspaper:** Approved as part of game presentation/narrative.
- **Shift timing:** User separately stated "shift timer only." Preserve a **shift-level clock/ending** while **not** imposing individual inspection deadlines. The exact clock semantics and pacing remain to be designed; do not silently adopt Claude's 120-second countdown.
- **Rebellion path:** An optional late-game storyline involves Mama infiltrating the bear capitol, killing the queen, dying as a martyr, and a bear revolution shown in a cutscene. User chose ending **B**: gameplay continues from **Button and Hazel's perspective** after Mama's death. This is not necessarily the only campaign ending.
- **Content tone:** User is comfortable with explicit depiction of fictional adult subjects, drugs/vaping as a potential storyline with school-aged Hazel, state violence, crime, and grim consequences. Actual specific encounters and portrayals still require design and consistent treatment; no specific incident has been mandated.

## Design implications

- Separate the government **gate entry** decision from **admission to Briarwood Refuge**.
- Staff continue refuge operations during Mama's checkpoint days; her management responsibilities remain, but she does not personally work full shifts at both sites each day.
- Document inspection must be readable and deliberative, without a countdown. Time costs can exist in the **larger daily schedule** only if explicitly developed later and consistent with approval.
- Never reuse the Restaurant Empire cub identities (Biscuit, Bruno, Mochi, Nugget) or the Tamagotchi cub Nova: those are distinct canons.
