# Research brief — Dwargon as inspiration for Honeyhold, and Queen Ursula (v0.1)

**Status:** USER-SUPPLIED REFERENCE + SOURCE-GROUNDED RESEARCH + UNAPPROVED DESIGN OPTIONS. 
**User request:** Develop **Queen Ursula individually**, and research a Honeyhold cave-bear city **inspired by Dwargon** from *That Time I Got Reincarnated as a Slime* (*Tensura*). Do **not** create or name any other individual character without explicit user approval. Do **not** copy copyrighted art, maps, characters or storylines.

## A. Existing approved game canon — do not rewrite

- *Unbearable Conditions*, set in **Honeyhold**, a bear-majority cave city with **seven connected districts**, explorable interiors, detailed NPC routines, minority cat/red panda populations and sharp inequality in housing, schooling, healthcare and services.
- Brown-bear royal dynasty. **Queen Ursula** is a **Kodiak brown bear**; publicly cuddly, affectionate and almost absurdly sweet, but ruthless when protecting her power.
- Constitutional monarchy with two major parties (names undecided) and royal ability to **indefinitely suspend most laws during declared emergencies**.
- All bears **walk on four legs**; visible emotional tears exist in-universe. Gameplay is detailed 2D pixel art.
- Gate work, child/family systems, Briarwood Refuge and political dilemmas must connect into a coherent world. The original Godot v0.1 Button/Hazel are **OLD / BAD / DO NOT USE**.
- **No new named supporting cast**: the former assistant-generated 20-character roster was rejected. Every new character must be approved individually. This brief addresses Queen Ursula **only**.

## B. What the Tensura sources actually support

1. **Large natural cave civilization**: the Armed Nation of Dwargon is built in natural caverns in the Great Canaat Mountains, north of the Jura Forest. Three principal cities include the capital Central, plus East and West. Mountain access is controlled by gates and forms a formidable natural defense. Source: https://tensura.fandom.com/wiki/Armed_Nation_of_Dwargon .
2. **Craft, industry and commerce**: the official Tensura world map describes Dwargon's core economy as mining, manufacturing of armaments and crafts, drawing foreign visitors interested in its technology/products. Source: https://www.ten-sura.com/map .
3. **Cultural scale and diversity**: early anime episode 4 introduces a prosperous, multi-species, carefully regulated free-trade city. Its king Gazel Dwargo is admired; public violence is forbidden and enforced militarily. These are attributes of *Dwargon*, not approved laws for Honeyhold. Source (anime dialogue archive): https://subs.yakuaru.com/books/that-time-i-got-reincarnated-as-a-slime/page/e4-in-the-kingdom-of-the-dwarves-71D ; official episode synopsis https://www.ten-sura.com/anime/tensura/story/no4 .
4. **King as institution**: official character page identifies Gazel as the third dwarven king, admired by subjects. His benevolent portrayal is a meaningful contrast to Ursula's approved ruthless politics. Source: https://www.ten-sura.com/character/gazel .
5. **Beyond one hall**: wiki background describes built-up cave environs, economic might, and a capital regulated through a narrow access system; fan sources are secondary and should not be overinterpreted into an exact Honeyhold map. Source: https://tensura.fandom.com/wiki/Armed_Nation_of_Dwargon .
6. **Source precision**: Dwargon is a nation with three principal cities. Honeyhold is one city with **seven approved districts**. Do not misstate these as the same scale or assume Honeyhold has three cities. Do not import Tensura's magic, dwarf technology, or laws automatically.

## C. Transferable design principles (recommendations, not canon)

**Keep:** huge mountain silhouette concealing a genuine metropolis; one or more defensible main gates; streets and plazas inside a vast natural cavern; carved residential terraces, enclosed public chambers and tunnel links; visibly functioning economy (craft, transport, workshops, heating, food markets); a monumental civic/royal zone; class/economic differences tied to physical location; recognizable movement between gate, home, refuge, schools and court.

**Change:** entirely original seven-district layout, bear/cat/red-panda ecology, quadrupedal architecture, Honey Marks economy, constitutional monarchy with an abusive emergency-power loophole, explicit immigration and refuge bureaucracy, independent media and Panda Mafia. Avoid cloning Dwargon's exact geography, character designs, visual maps, royal names, faction histories or anime screenshots into game assets.

**Proposed mountain geometry:**
- **Option A: One colossal hollow** — seven districts occupy different terraces and side caves around one gigantic interior plaza; visually closest to monumental Dwargon.
- **Option B: Seven connected great caverns** — each district is its own major chamber, linked by descending tunnels and control points; best for local cultural and economic differences.
- **Option C: Hybrid (recommended)** — one showpiece central cave containing the main civic/market districts, plus satellite cavern districts connected by broad ramps and passages. Exterior gate opens onto an immense urban interior. The Crown occupies a commanding rock shelf. Still exactly seven districts.

None of A/B/C is approved. Existing proposed district names from earlier design documents remain provisional.

**Practical topology and animals:** Wide sloped stone walkways and ramps; no human-only stair dependence; paw-accessible counters, low tables and resting furniture; low hanging signboards readable from animal eye height; passenger/freight elevators if technology supports them; robust ventilation and drainage. Wet habitats and food supply need ecological explanation; do not assume endless magic heating or underground farming without a selected technology model.

**Potential systemic gameplay:** The main gate is both the city's economic lifeline and a legal choke point. The Crown can restrict entry, trade or transit under indefinite emergency rule; shortages and prestige are then felt through district suppliers, schools, clinics, city jobs, family life and Briarwood allocations. A luxurious central palace may coexist with overheated or underheated residential tunnels. These are possible causal designs, not established events.

## D. Queen Ursula — *individual* design proposals

### Approved identity and portrayal

- Name: **Queen Ursula**.
- Species: **Kodiak brown bear**.
- Location: Honeyhold; residence/site of power TBD.
- Public image: cuddly, affectionate, extravagantly sweet, possibly actively cuddles subjects and cubs.
- Private/political nature: ruthless in protecting her authority.
- Constitutional powers: can suspend most laws indefinitely when a state of emergency is declared.
- Physiology and acting: **all fours**, genuine large Kodiak stature, bear muzzle and paws, emotional tears possible; avoid anthropomorphic standing/walking.
- Age, lineage, accession history, family members, relationships, physical marks, personal routines, and party ties are **not approved**.

### Proposed defining contradiction (choose one; not canon)

**A. Calculated mask:** Ursula deliberately performs maternal tenderness to manipulate public opinion; her affection is mostly tactical.

**B. Sincere affection, brutal paternalism:** She truly finds cubs adorable and wants subjects safe but believes her unlimited judgment overrides their rights; she can sincerely weep during a restrictive speech she authored.

**C. Possessive love (recommendation):** Ursula genuinely enjoys cuddling her subjects and seeks their approval, but sees Honeyhold as *hers*. She can comfort a distressed cub in public and sign an order that harms that child's household hours later, without perceiving inconsistency.

All three can generate dark satire; avoid reducing her to a purely random villain or copying King Gazel's personality.

### Three dimensions of physical/public spectacle (proposals)

1. **Royal appearance:** genuine massive adult Kodiak female, rich brown coat, heavily rounded shoulders and broad muzzle. Small metal circlet anchored appropriately for an animal; embroidered mantle draped across back, never human court gown or upright stance. Exact colors, size, eye characteristics, scars TBD.
2. **Public protocol:** royal "cuddle" visits, staged charity tours and grand declarations in a huge stone audience plaza. Distinguish propaganda from whether she feels genuine affection.
3. **Royal residence:** a high stone terrace opening over the city's central cavern, overlooking the main thoroughfare and gate; a palace connected via accessible broad ramps. Physical location not approved.

### Open Queen Ursula decisions — must ask before canonizing

- Is her maternal affection strategic, sincere-but-authoritarian, or possessive?
- Is the palace above a single cavern, integrated into the city, or deep in a separated royal cave?
- Ursula's age in bear-year terms, length of reign, and route to throne (inherited normally, childhood accession, coup, wartime emergency).
- Her relationship to existing parties and the precise formal act of declaring an emergency.
- Her current interest in Mama/Briarwood, if any, should be developed after approved history, not preinstalled as a scripted connection.
- Names/identities of any royal relatives, aides, guards or staff require **separate character creation and approval**.
