## Round 6 confirmed design constraints

1. **No bulk cast**: Every new character must be developed individually with explicit approval. The Round 5 goal of 12–20 deeply simulated recurring characters is a **future capacity target**, not authorization for assistant-made characters.
2. **Grandma**: formerly a **nursery teacher, fired**. No approved cause of firing. Her name is still undecided until the family's surname is developed.
3. **Government**: two major parties whose policy divide is welfare/civil liberties vs royal authority/security. **No party names have been approved**.
4. **Gate supervisor**: user approved an *archetype* who can self-interestedly help or betray Mama; **no named character is approved** yet.
5. **School**: Button/Hazel attend a public preschool with students from different social classes, but placement and access are sharply stratified by household wealth and district. Each sibling has separate teachers, classmates and interests; teacher/classmate identities are NOT established.
6. **Classmates**: previously proposed named classmates and older youth contacts are **REJECTED / N/A**. Invent no replacements until individual creation.
7. **Childcare replacement**: substantial multistep storyline including evidence, financial resources, employment coordination, available caretakers and trustworthy contacts. Emergency protection is distinct from arranging stable replacement.
8. **Godot v0.1 legacy naming collisions**: its old Button cub and old adult Hazel are **OLD, BAD, DO NOT USE**. Never adapt, reuse, rename into new cast members, or import into merged canon. Retain the original project snapshot for provenance only, without importing these characters. New family members need new stable IDs, not name-based matching.
9. **Cub autonomy and emotion**: siblings can hurt, fight, hate, reconcile or remain estranged; bears have visibly animated emotional tears; all bear movement is quadrupedal; continuous aging and developmental milestones remain required.

## Individual creation process (not a form to fill in all at once)

Pick **one** person to work on at a time. The user may choose to begin with Grandma's personal history (former nursery teacher, fired) or Queen Ursula's biography, or another role. Discuss and approve, as separate decisions, the individual's species/age, name and surname, physical look, temperament, relationships, home, occupation and prerequisites, history, current motives, and weekly routine. A detail moves into canon only after approval. Do not invent an automatic roster or write story scenes that silently assign identities to future NPCs.

**For implementation**: create character slots only for approved individuals. Other institutions can run with generic unnamed staffing and deferred data until their staff members are individually established. This document records no executed Godot code or newly built release.
