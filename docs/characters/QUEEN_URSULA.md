# Queen Ursula — Individual character dossier

**Status:** Individual character under development; **approved canon** is separated from **unapproved proposals**. No additional named characters may be created without the user's specific approval. See [Approved Decisions](../APPROVED_DECISIONS.md).

## Approved identity

- **Name:** Queen Ursula.
- **Species:** **Kodiak brown bear**; royal dynasty is brown bear.
- **Age at game start: 7 years old (APPROVED).** Crowned at 6 months, giving her **6 years and 6 months** on the throne at game start.
- **Cause of succession (A — APPROVED):** The **previous monarch died unexpectedly**, leading Ursula to inherit the throne at six months. The predecessor's identity, exact relationship to Ursula, cause of death and aftermath are **not decided**.
- **Setting:** Honeyhold, monumental hybrid mountain cave-city with seven connected districts.
- **Physiology:** Bears move on all fours and are capable of visible emotional tears in this universe.
- **Role and powers:** **The real sovereign with overwhelming practical authority from coronation at six months**, not merely a figurehead. Government officials administer and carry out her wishes but do not meaningfully veto or overrule them. Honeyhold retains constitutional-monarchy institutions and two parties, yet they cannot independently control her. She can suspend most laws indefinitely during declared emergencies.
- **Public personality:** Cuddly, affectionate, almost absurdly sweet.
- **Core psychological contradiction:** Ursula's love of her subjects and their adoration is **genuine, but possessive**. She views the populace almost as her own family or property. Rejecting her authority can provoke dangerous consequences; she protects her power ruthlessly.
- **Child accession (approved choice D; numeric age now approved):** Ursula was **crowned at exactly six months old**, in Honeyhold's accelerated bear-development system roughly equivalent to a **human three-year-old preschooler**. Courtiers reinforced her sense of entitlement.
- **Childhood upbringing (approved choice A):** She was **thoroughly spoiled**—constantly indulged, entertained and praised, and almost nobody dared say no.
- **Early governance (approved C, user correction stronger than original option):** **Ursula had real, effectively unchecked royal authority from six months old**. Adults handled paperwork and complex practical administration **for her, not over her**. Almost nobody dared refuse her, and no regency or ministry controlled her as a powerless child.

## Boundaries of the approved childhood

**Not established:** She was kept socially isolated, lacked genuine friendships, was lonely, lived under a strict ceremonial regime, lost a particular parent, had a particular regent, or experienced a particular traumatic event. The user approved the **spoiled upbringing**, NOT the alternative isolated-childhood options B or C. Do not silently substitute an invented causal trauma for her chosen history.

The identities of relatives, courtiers, household staff, opponents and any previously proposed NPCs are **not approved**. This dossier must never invent them.

## Psychological interpretation — proposals, not yet canon

- **Love interpreted as compliance:** years of deference may cause Ursula to see disobedience as a rejection of her affection.
- **Public warmth vs private demands:** she can be genuinely generous in one interaction yet impose draconian political consequences elsewhere. The contradiction is psychological, not simply a fake public persona.
- **Varied responses:** she need not be in a permanent tantrum state. Her reactions could depend on perceived loyalty, stakes, context, existing mood, relationship history, and the visibility of the slight.
- **Ruler with consequences:** when she signs a decree, it must change actual laws, departments, resident rights and family outcomes. An inaccurate Gazette headline must not be treated as objective truth.
- **Expressive presentation:** body posture, ear position, head movement, purring-like affectionate gestures where appropriate to bear anatomy, tears, forceful paw gestures. The exact art direction, coat, circlet, clothing and body size beyond Kodiak species are undecided.

## Illustrative character moments — not approved plot

1. Ursula enjoys a heartfelt affectionate meeting with subjects, then cannot comprehend why one of them declines a royal invitation.
2. A citizen criticizes a law that Ursula sincerely believes protects Honeyhold. She initially tries to persuade them with warmth; the refusal feels personal, and her response becomes politically dangerous.
3. A public celebration praises Ursula's kindness while the player can see how the emergency decree affects residents elsewhere in the city.

These are **fictional possibilities**, not mandatory scripted events or achievements already built.

## Accession-age decision — approved

The user explicitly selected **six months** as Queen Ursula's age when she became Queen. Because six months is Honeyhold's **preschool developmental stage** (roughly human age three), she did not need to personally do adult administrative tasks. **Subsequent clarification established that she had the real power from that very young age**: officials could handle administration, but not rule instead of her or veto her wishes. The mechanics for formalizing a cub's demands into official acts remain to be designed. Her **current age is now approved at seven years**.

**Do not create any additional characters** to explain succession, guardianship or government functioning. Do not assume a hidden regent or age-based check on her authority.

## Young Queen's actual power — C with explicit correction (APPROVED)

User statement: **"C but like she had lots of authority and like no one told her no and she has all the power really."** This OVERRIDES the softer version of C in which administrative officials could effectively rule instead of her.

**Binding interpretation:** Ursula was an unusually young ruler crowned at six months who **personally held ultimate practical power**. Nobody around her meaningfully told her no. Her indulgent upbringing was institutionally reinforced: preferences and childish demands were treated as sovereign wishes to be executed. Other government roles may perform technical work, but they do **not** hold overriding authority. Honeyhold can retain nominal constitutional forms and elected parties, but their ability to constrain Ursula in practice is extremely weak or nonexistent. Her indefinite emergency-law suspension power remains approved.

**Important limits on inference:** Do not give six-month-old Ursula adult intellect or professional skill merely because her commands were followed. Nor invent a named regent, royal relative, personal aide, resistance leader, specific childhood decree, or family tragedy. Formal wording of her ordinary constitutional powers remains a further design question.

**Next individual-development questions:** details of the predecessor's death (identity and manner unapproved), whether she retains a playful demand-making style in adulthood, how officials physically receive and implement her commands, and what she personally wants from subjects. Choose one topic at a time, without making new characters.
