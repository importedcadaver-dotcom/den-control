# Queen Ursula — Individual character dossier

**Status:** Individual character under development; **approved canon** is separated from **unapproved proposals**. No additional named characters may be created without the user's specific approval. See [Approved Decisions](../APPROVED_DECISIONS.md).

## Approved identity

- **Name:** Queen Ursula.
- **Species:** **Kodiak brown bear**; royal dynasty is brown bear.
- **Setting:** Honeyhold, monumental hybrid mountain cave-city with seven connected districts.
- **Physiology:** Bears move on all fours and are capable of visible emotional tears in this universe.
- **Role and powers:** Sovereign within a constitutional monarchy; the Queen can suspend most laws indefinitely during declared emergencies.
- **Public personality:** Cuddly, affectionate, almost absurdly sweet.
- **Core psychological contradiction:** Ursula's love of her subjects and their adoration is **genuine, but possessive**. She views the populace almost as her own family or property. Rejecting her authority can provoke dangerous consequences; she protects her power ruthlessly.
- **Child accession (approved choice D):** She became Queen unusually young, among royal courtiers who reinforced her sense of entitlement.
- **Childhood upbringing (approved choice A):** She was **thoroughly spoiled**—constantly indulged, entertained and praised, and almost nobody dared say no.

## Boundaries of the approved childhood

**Not established:** She was kept socially isolated, lacked genuine friendships, was lonely, lived under a strict ceremonial regime, lost a particular parent, had a particular regent, or experienced a particular traumatic event. The user approved the **spoiled upbringing**, NOT the alternative isolated-childhood options B or C. Do not silently substitute an invented causal trauma for her chosen history.

The identities of relatives, courtiers, household staff, opponents and any previously proposed NPCs are **not approved**. This dossier must never invent them.

## Psychological interpretation — proposals, not yet canon

- **Love interpreted as compliance:** decades (duration undetermined) of deference may cause Ursula to see disobedience as a rejection of her affection.
- **Public warmth vs private demands:** she can be genuinely generous in one interaction yet impose draconian political consequences elsewhere. The contradiction is psychological, not simply a fake public persona.
- **Varied responses:** she need not be in a permanent tantrum state. Her reactions could depend on perceived loyalty, stakes, context, existing mood, relationship history, and the visibility of the slight.
- **Ruler with consequences:** when she signs a decree, it must change actual laws, departments, resident rights and family outcomes. An inaccurate Gazette headline must not be treated as objective truth.
- **Expressive presentation:** body posture, ear position, head movement, purring-like affectionate gestures where appropriate to bear anatomy, tears, forceful paw gestures. The exact art direction, coat, circlet, clothing and body size beyond Kodiak species are undecided.

## Illustrative character moments — not approved plot

1. Ursula enjoys a heartfelt affectionate meeting with subjects, then cannot comprehend why one of them declines a royal invitation.
2. A citizen criticizes a law that Ursula sincerely believes protects Honeyhold. She initially tries to persuade them with warmth; the refusal feels personal, and her response becomes politically dangerous.
3. A public celebration praises Ursula's kindness while the player can see how the emergency decree affects residents elsewhere in the city.

These are **fictional possibilities**, not mandatory scripted events or achievements already built.
