## Additional Round 5 constraints

- **Cubs may fight, hate each other and hurt each other emotionally and physically.** Relationships can worsen, cause actual distress/injury, or eventually improve, but friendship/reconciliation is not guaranteed.
- **Bears have emotional tears** in the fictional universe. Make tears visually observable in sprite animation; bears remain quadrupedal.
- **Queen is Kodiak brown bear**. Not a polar bear or a separate royal species.
- **Grandma's cruelty is intentional and for amusement**, with coercive humiliating dares as user examples. The perpetrator is not an automatically sympathetic character.
- All prior approved rules (Button/Hazel names and ages, school, Mama's 3 gate/3 refuge/1 off schedule, Honey Marks, optional shift timer, etc.) remain in force.

## The assistant mistake after Round 5

The assistant drafted and committed a **20-character roster** (with invented names, species, occupations, biographies and relationship links), despite the user not consenting to bulk character authorship. The document called the roster "proposed" but still pushed an unwanted premise and then asked the user to approve it en masse. That overstepped the Round 5 headcount choice.

**Round 6 user correction:** "all of them gone dude i want to create them all individually!"

The invented cast was **withdrawn** from the current Round 6 document. No new named supporting character from it is canon. The user accepted other Round 6 recommendations with these clarifications:

- Queen name **Ursula** approved; Queen publicly cuddly/sweet but ruthless in protecting power.
- Grandma **formerly a nursery teacher who was fired**; cause and name not chosen.
- Preschool **A+B** (public mixed-class enrollment, but wealth/district-based placement inequalities).
- The previous classmates question is **N/A**; their invented names/roles are rejected.
- Gate supervisor is only a *behavior archetype* so far, not a named character.
- Names of political parties and Grandma remain undecided.
- Caregiver replacement is multistep, not instantly buyable.
- Original Godot v0.1 **Button (cub)** and **Hazel (adult)** are labeled **OLD / BAD / DO NOT USE** in merged canon. Source snapshot can remain as archive for provenance, but those characters cannot be imported/adapted into the new cast.

## Distinguish implementation from documentation

All of these files are text design records. They are **not evidence of playable characters**, implemented AI, or a completed Godot merge. Only approved people may be given canonical character records in the new game.
