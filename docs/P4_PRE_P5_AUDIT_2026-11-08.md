**Verification evidence:** [Actions run on inspected head](https://github.com/importedcadaver-dotcom/den-control/actions/runs/42534336468) succeeded. ## Executive decision

**P4 passes its current single-day anonymous training tests, but is not yet integration-ready for a repeatable seven-day gate/refuge/family game.** P5 can be designed, but **do not wire the P5 refuge to the current QA visitor records or directly expose the raw `place_in_refuge` primitive** until the identity/legal/case safeguards below are addressed. Do not claim P4 is untested or wholly broken; it has passing Godot CI. Do not confuse the current tests' limited coverage with full system readiness.

### Important distinctions

- **Confirmed by reading current code:** gate is initialized once per world lifetime; no reset/new per-day shift exists; P4's one adult-plus-cub fixture is a single persistent person; raw refuge placement only checks authorization and the existence of *some* case; case-result mirror compares only the reason; QA fixture snapshots are compared against whatever fixture file is installed at load time; only the home scene drives the cub autonomy timer.
- **Not dynamically reproduced here:** second gate day failure in Godot, corrupt-case mirror acceptance through `validate_state`, fixture-update invalidation through save restore, Windows UI clipping. Those follow from explicit code paths and require new behavioral tests before declaring their fixes verified.
- **Known P4 scope exclusions:** no Grandma or school offscreen childcare, public refuge budget, physical refuge operations, official Honeyhold law, wages, recurring authored applicant family, realistic multi-day branching consequences or Windows export. Those are **unimplemented**, not regressions.

|---|---|---|---|
| **BLOCKER G1 — Gate cannot run twice** | `gate_system.gd:30–33` rejects any `gate.started`; `world_state.gd:115–127` advances day but never archives/resets `state.gate`; `home_view.gd:102–109` also sees old `gate.complete`. Current tests end after the first gate day. | Subsequent gate days in the required 3/3/1 rotation cannot begin. | Model date-keyed work shifts, archive results, create one active shift per scheduled gate day, and regression-test day 0 gate → day 1 refuge → day 2 gate. | **BLOCKER G2 — Person/party identity collapsed** | `gate_training_cases.json` QA-04 says *adult and cub* but supplies one `subject_id`; `gate_system.gd:44–62` creates exactly one subject per ticket. | The cub has no individual legal/guardian/placement identity, making P5 family custody outcomes impossible. | Separate **encounter/case** from **individual participants** with stable IDs, party relationships and separately assessed legal statuses. Anonymous QA people remain unnamed fixtures, not new canon cast. |
| **BLOCKER G3 — Raw refuge placement is too permissive** | `world_state.gd:197–204` requires an authorized entry and any registered `case_id`, not a matching case for the person, refuge eligibility or available bed. | A character can be placed using an unrelated case, with no capacity or welfare basis. | P5 owns `check_placement_eligibility` + capacity/bed assignment + costs + independent placement decision, and validates subject-case link and entry/legal policy. Never expose the raw operation directly to UI. |
| **HIGH G4 — QA content couples to persistent world** | `gate_system.gd:34–74` always loads six QA fixtures and enables `qa_mode`; `gate_system.gd:205–220` validates saved queue against the **current** fixture file; `gate_training_cases.json` stores expected verdicts/expiry dates. | P5 may accidentally treat QA visitors as real refuge residents; changing fixture data can invalidate earlier saves; later dates would require re-evaluating expiry rather than reusing fixed verdicts. | Separate fixture/test mode from production applicants; store immutable versioned policy/case snapshots with save data, and compute effective validity by day. QA data must never automatically become canon citizens. |
| **HIGH G5 — Incomplete case-result consistency validation** | `gate_system.gd:230–248` validates decision vs entry state, but the `case_records[case_id].gate_training_result` mirror is compared with the decision on **reason only**. | The copied result can disagree on subject, status or checks and still be insufficiently cross-validated. Particularly risky once P5 reads case outcomes. | Make one authoritative decision record with references; require full canonical cross-check of the mirror or remove the duplicate record. Regression-test conflicting statuses/checks. |
| **HIGH G6 — Home autonomy freezes during work** | `home_view.gd:30–39,165–174` owns the only calling timer for `world.tick_home()`; gate scene does not advance family or childcare; P4 checkpoint explicitly acknowledges this. | Mama's six-month-old cubs have no simulated needs/supervision during a potentially long gate shift; P5 will compound the freeze. | Establish a single world-time service and offscreen-simulation policy; test a work shift advancing cub needs and separately model Mama/Grandma/school custody coverage without automatically inventing incidents. |
| **HIGH G7 — Midway shift initialization is not atomic** | `gate_system.gd:40–71` advances the calendar phase, enables QA, registers cases and subjects and changes gate state before `world.start_shift()` has definitely succeeded. | A malformed fixture or failed clock start can leave a partially initialized world. | Prevalidate all fixture/case/shift prerequisites and stage mutations; commit or roll back as one transition. Test malformed case at index 2 and clock-start failure. |
| **HIGH G8 — Current gate tests are narrower than a real investigation loop** | `gate_system.gd:118–147` allows a fixed list of reasons; test fixtures carry preselected `expected_status` and `expected_reason`; only two questions and two verification types exist. | Cases are largely static rule matching; an incorrect decision is stored but has no persistent social, salary, administrative or later refuge consequences. | Keep the training audit, but add at least one multi-step authored or anonymous case with evidence state, lawful versus humane outcomes, deferred follow-up and observable consequences in P5/P6. No new named person without approval. |
| **MEDIUM G9 — Holding a case requires completed verification** | `gate_system.gd:123–127` requires `requires_check` before **any** status including HOLD. | The player cannot elect a provisional hold specifically because evidence is still missing. | Define HOLD as a pending-investigation state; allow reasoned hold without already-completed verification when policy supports it. Authorization/exception may require verified evidence. |
| **MEDIUM G10 — Adult-with-cub image is misleading** | `gate_view.gd:279–282` passes `accompanied_minor` as the small-body flag to a **single** draw call. | In QA-04 the adult visually shrinks; no independent cub appears, despite the written case and four-legged bear family presentation requirements. | Render adult and cub as separate figures and stable identities; verify screenshot at 960×540. |
| **MEDIUM G11 — Fixed-size UI and incomplete real-world usability evidence** | `gate_view.gd:60–147,270–308` positions a 960×540 desk and 292px sidebar, with 800px scroll-stack; screenshot/readability never manually inspected on Windows. | Button controls may be hard to discover, text may clip or require hidden scrolling, and the stamp is a decorative drawing paired with ordinary buttons rather than a physically manipulable object. | Manual screenshot + Windows interaction review; keyboard access; visible scroll hints, better evidence comparison and a clearer primary decision panel. This can be a P6 polish gate, not a P5 data blocker. |
| **MEDIUM G12 — Some work clocks and budgets are still placeholders** | `gate_system.gd:13` gives 1,200s provisional training time; `world_state.gd` retains null HM balances and debt. The timer's `menus` pause mechanism is supported but not wired to an actual settings/menu UI. | P5 cannot deduct public supplies/care costs without a consciously configured test account; players may perceive inconsistent pause behavior. | Initialize **explicitly labeled provisional test figures**, not new canon money; test public-vs-private ledger separation, open Settings and timer pause behavior. |
| **MEDIUM G13 — Early closure has no downstream case lifecycle** | `gate_system.gd:149–167` records `gate.skipped`, leaving cases without entry decisions. | P5 could mistake unanswered cases for denials or have no mechanism to resume/review them. | Keep pending/unprocessed status distinct from held and denied; test what happens to skipped applicants on the next day and to refuge inquiries. |
| **MEDIUM G14 — Save-state compatibility and QA cleanup** | `SAVE_VERSION=4` and `GateSystem.validate` require the present fixture dataset; developer saves were intentionally incompatible across P2/P3/P4. | When P5 introduces persistent residents and real content, another schema bump could destroy newly tested continuity. | Establish schema versioning/migration or explicit stable save compatibility from first production release. Keep dev-only fixtures isolated from real save-world records. |

## Regression tests to add before P5 is declared integration-ready

1. **Repeated rotation:** complete first gate workday, advance through refuge duty, start another gate workday, verify distinct date-keyed queues and preservation of first-day cases.
2. **Immutable historical evidence:** save completed day-one gate, change active future training rules, load old case without rewriting historical outcome or invalidating save by accident.
3. **Party with cub:** one case includes adult and cub as two independent anonymous stable IDs; check separate custody/entry/placement states, visual render of both, and no automatic custody assignment.
4. **Case ownership:** authorized applicant A cannot be placed in Briarwood on applicant B's case without an explicitly valid cross-case legal basis.
5. **Capacity and ledger:** deny placement beyond capacity, charge only the refuge account, reject overdraft, verify no invented initial HM balances.
6. **Pending vs held vs denied:** early shift close preserves pending cases; a held case remains reviewable and doesn't magically acquire refusal or residence status.
7. **Corrupt duplicate-result detection:** mutate one field of `case_records[*].gate_training_result` (status, subject or verification) and assert failure or canonical reconstruction.
8. **Atomic shift opening:** malformed second fixture and no clock configuration leave no partly started shift/case/QA person records behind.
9. **Offscreen household:** after 10, 20 or 60 in-game minutes away from the home scene, Button/Hazel need and intention state progress via the world-time service; supervision status is explicit and separate from legal parentage.
10. **Save/load across handoff:** gate decisions → separate refuge eligibility → placement decision → hands-on care → save and reload, verifying same identities and public finance audit trail.
11. **UI manual audit:** real screenshot(s) at 960×540 and common Windows scaling, confirm document readability, touch/click targets, sidebar scrolling, no clipped actions and accurate adult/cub depiction.
12. **Anti-duplication:** repeated placement or care click cannot create extra beds, repeated HM charges or duplicate residents; one time advancement cannot produce multiple wages.

## Readiness decision and scope of upcoming work

**Gate-only training milestone:** conditionally accepted as a functioning prototype with passing tests.  
**Gate-to-refuge handoff:** **NOT READY** for real resident/custody/financial consequences until G2–G5 and G3 guardrails are in place.  
**Seven-day rotation:** **NOT READY** until G1 is fixed.  
**World/family time integration:** **NOT READY** until G6 and the planned school/Grandma schedule are designed and verified.  
**P5 may start after a targeted P4.1 repair sprint**, or its first technical substage may explicitly include those repairs. Do not copy P4 QA fixtures straight into Briarwood as canonical residents. QA content is allowed to remain for regression tests.

## Canon and user-approval constraints

- Preserve Mama, the six-month-old biological littermates Button and Hazel, fired ex-nursery-teacher Grandma and Queen Ursula. Do not create a supporting character roster; new named individuals are user-approved one at a time.
- Grandma's deliberate cruelty, Mama's awareness, and childcare dependency **cannot be silently rewritten** as loving or harmless supervision. This audit does not invent new mistreatment incidents.
- Existing rulebook and pass values are **QA-only**, not approved Honeyhold legislation. Any actual gate/refuge case or legal system should be versioned as game content without automatically importing QA truth flags into canon.
- Preserve the broad seven-district hybrid Dwargon-inspired city, 3 gate / 3 refuge / 1 off work rotation, household vs public budgets, resident legal rights, continuous aging, and individual autonomy.
- **No code, scenes, characters or P5 mechanics were changed in this audit.** The report is a checkpoint for a targeted repair sprint and integration work.
